## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Bose smart speakers and soundbars set up with the Bose app (Smart Soundbar, Soundbar 700/900, Home Speaker 500, Portable Smart Speaker, Music Amplifier…) found on the local network through the mDNS discovery of Gladys (`_bose-passport._tcp`), plus IP addresses typed in the configuration; each speaker is identified by its GUID and found again after an address change.
- Bose account sign-in with e-mail and password (the speakers only accept a token of their account), token kept in `/data` and refreshed before it expires; a refused password is not tried again until the configuration changes. Commands and states stay on the local network.
- One device per speaker: power, volume, mute, play, pause, previous, next, playback state (the Music box of the dashboard), source (TV, optical, analogue, AUX and the presets), now playing, artist, and depending on the model bass, midrange, treble, centre, surround, height, subwoofer, dialogue mode, battery and charging.
- States pushed by the speakers over their local WebSocket, no polling; a local or unreachable badge per speaker, automatic reconnection.
- Dashboard widget "Bose speaker": what is playing with its cover, state, source, volume, battery, group, and the buttons previous, play/pause, next and standby.
- Scene triggers: playback started, playback stopped, source changed, speaker unreachable.
- Scene actions: play a preset, group speakers, take a speaker out of its group.
- Configuration button "Test the account and search the speakers".
- Texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
